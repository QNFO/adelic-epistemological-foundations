## Core Claim

The physically accessible base field of physics is **ℚ (the rational numbers)**, not ℝ (the real numbers). Ostrowski's theorem then demands that **all** p-adic completions of ℚ be physically meaningful.

## Why It Matters

If correct, this transforms the Adelic Physics Program from "mathematical speculation" to "physical programme." Physics has arbitrarily restricted itself to one completion (ℝ) out of the infinitely many that Ostrowski's theorem classifies.

## Key Supporting Arguments

1. **No experiment distinguishes ℝ from ℚ.** Every measurement produces a rational number. Every computer simulation uses finite precision. No physical prediction has ever required a non-computable real number.

2. **Computable reals are sufficient.** Every number in any physics paper (π, e, α, masses) is computable — approximable by a finite rational procedure.

3. **Gisin's argument:** Real numbers are the hidden variables of classical mechanics. The infinite digits of x(0) encode all future states — structurally identical to hidden variables.

4. **Bekenstein bound:** Finite spacetime regions cannot contain infinite information. Real numbers require infinite specification.

5. **Constructive number theory:** The Laws of Form Number Builder constructs ℚ from distinction primitives in 4 steps. Step 6 (Monna-map → ℝ) is lossy — non-computable reals are projection artifacts.
